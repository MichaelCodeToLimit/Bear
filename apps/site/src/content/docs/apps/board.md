Board is a whiteboard for sketching, brainstorming and planning. It is built on [Excalidraw](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw), an open-source drawing tool, and it saves automatically.

## Your boards

Board opens on a gallery of all your whiteboards. Start a new one, or click an existing one to carry on where you left off.

## Draw

The toolbar has everything you need to get an idea out of your head:

- **Pens** for freehand drawing.
- **Shapes** and **arrows** to build diagrams and connect them up.
- **Text** for labels and headings.
- **Sticky-note style shapes** for brainstorms.

Fonts ship inside the app, so your board looks the same on every computer.

## Share a picture

Export a board as a **PNG** or **SVG** image to drop into a document, a slide or an email.

## Where boards live

Boards are stored in Bear's data folder on your computer. Saves are written safely, so a crash cannot leave a half-written file.
