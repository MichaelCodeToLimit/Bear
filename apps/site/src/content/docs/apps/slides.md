Slides is Bear's presentation maker. Build a deck, rehearse it, and present it full screen. It works with `.pptx` files.

## Open a file

Choose **File, then Open**, or double-click a `.pptx`. Bear brings in text, shapes, pictures, backgrounds and speaker notes from presentations made in other apps.

## Build your slides

Thumbnails run down the left side. Click one to edit that slide, and drag thumbnails to reorder them. **New slide** offers layouts: title, title and content, section, two content, title only and blank.

On each slide you can add:

- **Text boxes** that you click and type into.
- **Shapes**: rectangle, rounded rectangle, oval, triangle, arrow and line, each with its own text, fill and outline.
- **Pictures**, inserted from a file or pasted in.

Type your talking points into the speaker notes for each slide.

## Move things around

Select, move and resize anything on the slide. Snap guides help you line things up with the slide centre and edges. You can select several items at once, copy, paste and duplicate them, change their stacking order, and align them to each other. Undo and redo are always there.

## Pick a look

Choose one of six themes. Shapes coloured from the theme follow it when you switch. You can also give any single slide its own background.

## Present

Start the show **from the beginning (F5)** or **from the current slide (Shift+F5)**. During the show:

- Use the arrow keys, Space or a click to move forward.
- Type a slide number and press Enter to jump there.
- Press **B** for a black screen.
- Press **Esc** to leave.

The slide sorter gives you a bird's-eye view of the whole deck.

## Save and print

Save as **.pptx**, export to PDF, or print. New decks can start from a template.
