Mail lets you read and send email from your own accounts without leaving Bear. It works with any provider that offers IMAP and SMTP, including Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, Fastmail and many more.

## Add your account

1. Open Mail and choose to add an account.
2. Type your **email address** and the **name** people should see when you write to them.
3. Bear recognises many common providers and fills in the server settings for you. If it does not recognise yours, it makes a best guess, and you can check the **Advanced** section to correct the incoming (IMAP) and outgoing (SMTP) servers.
4. Sign in. Some providers let you **sign in through your browser**, so Bear never sees your password. Others ask for an **app password**, a special password you create in your provider's security settings. Where one is needed, the dialog links to the right page.

Your password is encrypted on your computer and only sent to your mail provider. You can add more than one account, and you can edit or remove an account later from the menu next to it.

## Read your mail

Folders are listed on the left, with unread counts. The message list sits in the middle and the open message on the right. Click **Load older messages** at the bottom of a long list to go further back. Use the search box to search the current folder.

For your safety, remote images in a message are **blocked until you allow them**, because they can reveal when you opened an email. Choose **Show pictures** on messages you trust. Message bodies are cleaned and shown in a locked-down view, so scripts in an email cannot run.

## Write and reply

Click **New mail** or press **Ctrl+N**. Fill in To, and Cc and Bcc if you need them, add a subject, attach files, and press **Send** or **Ctrl+Enter**.

On an open message you can **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward**.

Drafts are saved on your computer as you type, so if you close the window you can come back to them under **Unsent drafts**.

## Keep tidy

On any message you can **Archive**, **Delete** (the Del key works too), **Flag** or **Mark as unread**, or move it to another folder from the right-click menu.

## Attachments

Attachments appear under the message. Save them to your computer, or open the ones that Bear understands (such as a `.docx` or `.xlsx`) straight in the right app.
