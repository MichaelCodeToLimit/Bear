Write is Bear's word processor. It is built for letters, reports, resumes and everything in between, and it works with `.docx` files.

## Open a file

Choose **File, then Open**, or double-click a `.docx` in your file manager. Write also opens `.html`, `.txt` and `.md` files. Pictures, tables and links come along with the document.

## Format your text

The ribbon has Home, Insert, Layout and View tabs.

- **Home** has fonts and sizes, bold, italic, underline and strikethrough, subscript and superscript, text colour and highlight. It also has styles (headings, quote, code), bullets and numbering with nesting, and alignment.
- **Insert** adds tables, pictures, links, page breaks and horizontal lines.
- **Layout** sets Letter or A4, portrait or landscape, and margins.
- **View** changes the zoom, turns on focus mode, and switches between the light and dark theme.

## Work with tables

Pick a table size straight from the grid, or use **Insert table** for exact numbers. Once your cursor is in a table, a Table tab appears with styles (grid, accent header, banded and minimal), cell shading, vertical alignment, a header row or column, merge and split, and distribute columns.

## Add pictures

Insert a picture from a file, paste one from the clipboard, or drag it onto the page. Any common image format works, and very large photos are scaled down so your document stays a sensible size.

## Find and replace

Press **Ctrl+F** to find text and **Ctrl+H** to find and replace it.

## Save and share

- **Save as .docx** is the usual choice, and other word processors can open the file. You can also save as `.html` or `.txt`.
- **AutoSave** keeps a named document up to date while you work.
- **Export to PDF** and **Print** are on the File menu.
- Bear warns you before closing a window that has unsaved changes.

## Start from a template

New documents can begin from a letter, resume or report template, so you are not staring at a blank page.
