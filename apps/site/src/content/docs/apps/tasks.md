Tasks is a to-do list that grows with you, from a quick shopping list to a plan with columns. It saves automatically.

## Smart lists

The left side has built-in views:

- **My Day** for what you plan to do today.
- **Important** for tasks you starred.
- **Planned** for tasks with a due date.
- **All tasks** for everything in one place.

Below them are **your own lists**. Make one for each project or area of life.

## Add a task

Type a task and press Enter. Open it to add:

- **Steps**, a small checklist inside the task.
- A **due date**.
- **Notes** for extra detail.
- **Importance**, to mark it as a priority.

Tick the circle when it is done.

## Board view

Switch a list to a board to see your tasks as cards in columns. Drag a card from one column to another as work moves along. Moving a card to **Done** completes the task.

## Undo

Deleted something by mistake? Use **undo** straight after to bring it back.

## Where your tasks live

Tasks are kept in Bear's data folder on your computer and written safely, so a crash cannot corrupt them. A Tasks list can also be linked to a [Sites](../sites/) home page.
