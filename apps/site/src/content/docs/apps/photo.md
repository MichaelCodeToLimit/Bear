Photo is the first app in Bear Studio, Bear's creative apps. Use it to retouch pictures, build up an image in layers or make a quick graphic. It comes with the full Bear download and with Bear Studio.

## Layers

Each image is a stack of layers. Give a layer a **blend mode** and an **opacity**, hide it, rename it or drag it up and down the stack.

## Select

- **Marquee** for rectangles and ellipses.
- **Lasso** to draw around an area by hand.
- **Magic wand** to pick up similar colours with one click.

## Paint and draw

Use the **brush**, **eraser**, **fill** and **gradient** tools, draw **shapes**, and add **text**.

## Adjust and filter

- **Adjustments:** brightness/contrast, levels, curves, hue/saturation, invert and black & white.
- **Filters:** blurs, sharpen, unsharp mask, noise and pixelate.

## Change the image

Crop, rotate, flip, resize the image or the canvas, and trim away empty edges.

## Open, save and export

- **Open** .psd, PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP and AVIF files.
- **Save** as .bearphoto, Photo's own format that keeps every layer, or as .psd.
- **Export** a flat PNG, JPG or WebP.

## Crash recovery

Photo keeps a recovery copy as you work. If the app closes unexpectedly, it offers to bring your image back the next time you open it.
