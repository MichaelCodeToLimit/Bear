Sheets is Bear's spreadsheet. It handles budgets, trackers and number crunching, and it works with `.xlsx` and `.csv` files.

## Open a file

Choose **File, then Open**, or double-click an `.xlsx`. Values, formulas, styles, column widths, merged cells and frozen panes all come across. You can open `.csv` files too.

## Type a formula

Start a cell with `=` and write a formula, for example `=SUM(B2:B10)` or `=IF(C2>100, "Over", "OK")`. Sheets understands the functions most people use, including SUM, IF, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, MATCH, SUMIFS, MAX, TEXT and PMT. Formulas can point at other sheets in the same workbook, and Sheets shows proper error values and catches formulas that refer to themselves.

Need a quick total? Select your numbers and use **AutoSum**. The status bar also shows the sum, average and count of whatever you have selected.

## Fill, copy and sort

- Drag the **fill handle** at the corner of a cell to continue a series. "Item 1" becomes "Item 2", and formulas are copied with their references adjusted.
- Copy, cut and paste keep relative references working.
- **Sort** a column from A to Z or Z to A.

## Numbers that look right

Pick a format for currency, percent, dates or decimals. Bear also notices as you type: `15%` becomes a percentage, `$4.50` becomes currency and `2026-10-14` becomes a date.

## Shape the grid

Insert or delete rows and columns and your formulas adjust. Resize columns by dragging, or auto-fit them. Freeze panes keep headings in view, and merged cells, text overflow and wrapping are all supported. The grid is fast enough for 100,000 rows. Use the tabs at the bottom to add, rename and reorder sheets.

## Chart your data

Select a range and insert a column, bar, line or pie chart. Charts are kept when you save to `.xlsx` and show up again when you reopen the file in Bear.

## Save and print

Save as **.xlsx** or **.csv**, export to PDF, or print. New workbooks can start from a budget, invoice or task tracker template.
